# Role profiles

## AI healthcare / AI drug discovery

Prioritize biomedical problem framing, data pipelines, multimodal or imaging
workflows, reproducibility, candidate prioritization, validation loops, and
software/automation delivery. Avoid claiming clinical validity without evidence.

## Bioinformatics / computational biology

Prioritize omics, single-cell, reproducible workflows, R/Python/Linux,
statistical reasoning, data quality, visualization, and collaboration with wet
lab or clinical teams.

## Medical AI solutions

Prioritize data-to-decision workflows, image processing, structured outputs,
workflow reliability, requirements decomposition, explainability, and
stakeholder communication.

## Academic CV

Prioritize research questions, methods, publications with exact status,
funding, teaching, mentoring, and scholarly service. Do not force an
industry-style summary unless requested.
