# Content rules

## Truthfulness

- Use only facts supplied by the user or source document.
- Preserve the original status of publications: submitted, under review,
  revision, accepted, or published.
- Attribute grants, patents, products, code, and metrics only to the role and
  team that the user confirms.
- Ask for clarification or use a visible placeholder when a claim is material
  and unverified.

## Industry framing

- Lead with the target role, relevant domain, and transferable capabilities.
- Convert research methods into decision, automation, analysis, validation,
  delivery, or collaboration value where the evidence supports it.
- Prefer outcome-oriented bullets: action + scope + evidence + result.
- Do not use vague self-ratings such as “proficient” unless backed by a project
  or objective evidence.

## ATS and layout

- Use standard headings: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects,
  Achievements, Languages, Links.
- Keep a single column, readable font, and selectable text for ATS versions.
- Put contact details and critical education/skills on the first page.
- Render the final PDF and inspect every page for clipping, overflow, and
  awkward page breaks.

## Privacy

- Keep reusable examples synthetic.
- Exclude sensitive demographic fields by default.
- Treat a photo as an optional local input; do not commit it.
